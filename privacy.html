Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Tool Hub ("we", "our", "the app") handles information when you use our tools. We've built Tool Hub to work locally on your device wherever possible.

Information We Collect

Most tools in Tool Hub process your files (images, PDFs, text) directly on your device — this data is not uploaded to any server.

We use Google Analytics to understand general usage patterns (such as which tools are opened and how often), which may collect standard technical data like device type, browser, and approximate location.

Advertising

Tool Hub displays ads from third-party ad networks to keep the tools free to use. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads. We do not control the specific data collection practices of third-party ad providers.

Data Storage

  • Tool preferences and settings are stored locally on your device using browser storage.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Choices

You can clear your locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app. You may also opt out of personalized advertising through your device or browser settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
